
## Definition of the problem:

**Questions**:
1.What are the categories and regions with the highest revenue?
2.In which categories do discounts increase sales the most?
3.Do high discounts generate more revenue or more units?
4.In which regions is the greatest demand concentrated?

**Business task**: Understand sales behavior to optimize marketing, discount and geographic expansion strategies.
**Stakeholders**: Marketing team, sales team, regional offices.


**Dataset used**: "Amazon Sales Dataset" by Kaggle (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/aliiihussain/amazon-sales-dataset )
**Columns included**: order_id, order_date, product_category, total_revenue, quantity_sold, discount_percent, customer_region, payment_method, rating, review_count

**Limitations of the dataset**:
-Inconsistent product attributes (same product_id with different categories and prices).
-Lack of real customer_id (can't do analysis of individual customers).
-Synthetic data.
