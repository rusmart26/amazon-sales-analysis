
# Amazon Sales Analysis

**Data Analyst Junior Portfolio Project**  
**Author:** Ruslana Martiniuc  
**Date:** March 2026

### Project Objective
Comprehensive analysis of Amazon sales data to identify top-performing categories and regions, evaluate discount effectiveness, understand payment behavior, and provide actionable recommendations for marketing, pricing, and geographic expansion.

### Key Findings
- **Beauty** leads in both revenue ($5.56M) and units sold, but the gap with other categories is minimal.
- Revenue is distributed almost identically across the four regions → excellent geographical diversification and low risk.
- The **1-10% discount range** is the clear "sweet spot": it doubles sales volume while maximizing revenue.
- Payment infrastructure is very well integrated (Wallet is #1, but difference with least used method is only 198 transactions).
- Seasonal campaigns in 2023 were slightly less effective than in 2022.
